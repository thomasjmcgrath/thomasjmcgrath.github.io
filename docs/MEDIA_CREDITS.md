Fonts and images are served locally. All seven catalog profiles have individually identified archive images, descriptions and visible source credits. The dates above are source publication dates unless a photograph date is specified. No external font tracker or automatic video/map embed. Profiles without an image retain an explicitly labelled icon fallback. Archive images do not establish current animal residence or public-viewing arrangements.

The maintained Sites integration licence is `scripts/vite/sites-vite-plugin.LICENSE`. Other dependencies retain their upstream licences. There is no blanket relicensing of third-party assets.

- `content/world-land.json`: equirectangular paths from Natural Earth's public-domain 1:110m land GeoJSON. [Source](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson). [Terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). Geometry uses actual longitude/latitude.
